## 📅 Managing Appointments

Every time someone books on your website, a card appears in your dashboard. Here are the 3 main actions:

### 1. Confirming an Appointment (Green Button)
* **When to use:** When you are available at the requested date and time.
* **What happens:**
  1. Click **Confirm**.
  2. The appointment moves to the **Confirmed** tab and the calendar locks the slot so nobody else can take it.
  3. WhatsApp automatically opens with a pre-written message confirming the date, time, and service. Tap **Send** in WhatsApp!

### 2. Rescheduling an Appointment (Blue Button)
* **When to use:** When you are busy at that exact hour, but you want to offer the client a different time or day.
* **What happens:**
  1. Click **Reschedule**.
  2. Pick your proposed new date and time slot.
  3. *(Optional)* Add a friendly note (e.g., *"Good morning dear, 10am is booked, can we do 2:00pm?"*).
  4. Click **Send Reschedule Proposal**. WhatsApp opens with the proposal ready to send.

### 3. Declining an Appointment (Red Button)
* **When to use:** When you cannot take the client (e.g. traveling, family emergency, or fully booked).
* **What happens:**
  1. Click **Decline**.
  2. Choose a reason (*Fully Booked*, *Salon Closed*, *Emergency*).
  3. Submit. WhatsApp opens with a polite, professional apology note.

### 4. Direct WhatsApp Chat
* On any appointment card, click the green **WhatsApp** button to immediately chat with that client.
* Use this to ask questions about hair length, color choices, or pre-braiding hair prep.

## 💇 Adding & Editing Services & Pricing (₦)

To change prices or add new braid styles:
1. Tap the **Services** button at the top of your dashboard.
2. **To change a price:** Find the hairstyle, tap **Edit**, type the new price in Naira (₦), and click **Save**.
3. **To add a new hairstyle:** Tap **+ Add New Service**, enter the name (e.g. *French Curl Braids*), price, styling duration in hours, and category.
4. **To temporarily pause a service:** Toggle it to inactive so clients won't see it until you turn it back on.

## 📸 Portfolio & Hairstyle Gallery

To show clients your latest braiding work:
1. Tap the **Portfolio** button at the top of your dashboard.
2. Tap **Upload Media** and pick photos or videos from your phone or computer.
3. Add a tag (e.g., *Knotless*, *Box Braids*, *Boho*, *Twists*) so clients can filter styles easily.
4. Tap the **Star ⭐ (Featured)** icon if you want this picture displayed on your website's front home page!

---

## 🚫 Days Off & Blocking Hours (Unavailability)

Whenever you need time off for church, resting, travel, or personal errands:
1. Tap the **Unavailability** button at the top of your dashboard.
2. **To block an entire day off:** Select the date (e.g. Sunday or a holiday), leave the time slot blank, and click **Block Date**.
3. **To block just morning or afternoon:** Select the date and pick the specific hours (e.g. 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM).
4. **To remove a block:** Scroll to your list of blocked dates and tap the red trash can 🗑️ icon. The slot opens up immediately.

---

## 🔔 Getting Phone Alerts & Installing App

Get sound or vibration alerts whenever a customer books:
1. Tap **Push Alerts** on your dashboard and click **Enable Push Notifications**.
2. When your phone asks *"Allow notifications?"*, tap **Allow**.
3. **Add to Phone Screen:**
   * **On iPhone:** Open the website in Safari, tap the Share button (square with arrow pointing up), and choose **"Add to Home Screen"**.
   * **On Android:** Open in Google Chrome, tap the 3 dots in the top right, and choose **"Install App"**.

---

## 🔐 Changing Your Password & Security

1. Tap **Security** at the top of your dashboard.
2. Enter your new password and confirm it.
3. Tap **Update Password**.
4. Always tap **Sign Out** if you are accessing your dashboard from a shared or public computer.

## 💡 Top 5 Tips for Helen

1. **Keep WhatsApp Open on Your Phone:** All confirmation and reschedule messages are designed to be sent through WhatsApp with 1 tap.
2. **Watch for Red Conflict Banners:** If you see a red warning on an appointment card, it means that time was already booked or blocked off. Click **Reschedule** to suggest another time.
3. **Use the "Admin Guide" In-App Button Anytime:** Whenever you forget how to do something, tap the amber **Admin Guide** button in your dashboard header to open the interactive tutorial!
4. **Feature Your Best Photos:** Clients love seeing real photos. Uploading 2-3 new styles every week keeps your homepage looking fresh.
5. **Mark Completed:** At the end of the day, mark today's finished appointments as *Completed* so your active dashboard stays neat and focused.
